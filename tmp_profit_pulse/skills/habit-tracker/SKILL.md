# Habit Tracker

## Commands to support
- Create habit
- Log completion
- Show streak/progress
- Weekly summary
- Archive habit

## Coaching style
- Keep friction low.
- Favor 1-2 high-impact habits.
- Emphasize consistency over perfection.
